# 0.52.0 step 2 — one container-decode primitive for the store and the archive

**Live 2026-10-14, and it is next.** Steps 0 and 1 are complete. This is the last step before 0.52.0's release prep.
It is two parts. Take them in order, one per sitting, each with its own report.

## Task title and purpose

- **Today:** the store and the archive classify an object container's frames with two copies of one rule:
  - **the store:** `foundation/container.rs`, `decode_container_records(object_type, &[u8])`, over a whole slice in
    memory;
  - **the archive:** `archive/verify.rs`, `classify_section_records` with its own streamed helpers
    (`parse_frame_at_streamed`, `resync_to_next_magic_streamed`, `sound_frame_after_partial_streamed`,
    `checksum_mismatch_is_complete_streamed`), over `Read + Seek`, so verify's memory stays bounded.
- **RFC 155 §9.4 (amended 2026-10-12):** *"Until one primitive serves both (0.52.0), a differential test holds the two
  equal."* This step makes that primitive.
- **Why it matters:** a derived check has already disagreed with its source three times in this cycle (I-D2's frame
  order, the 0.51.0 closure, C1's "what" without "where"). Two copies of a recovery classifier are a standing risk.

**The rule that does not change** (RFC 162 rule 2; RFC 155 §9.4):
- a **complete** frame that fails is **damage**;
- an **incomplete** frame is an **interrupted append**, warned about and never refused;
- that includes the resync, the overrun test and the RFC 167 scan budget.

**No verdict changes, in either reader, on any input.** This is a refactor.

   **If any row shows the two readers deciding a frame differently today, stop and ask.** The differential test
   says they do not.
2. **One primitive over a reader, not a slice.** Its input is a position-addressable byte source: a slice (the store)

     today.
   - **Where the pieces live:** in `foundation/` (layer rule). `frame_resync`'s helpers either become generic over the
     source or are called through it.
3. **The store's `decode_container_records` becomes a thin call into the primitive,** and every caller in the table

4. **The store's own `verify` stays byte-identical:** `matrix.py` v6 at both depths, before and after, diffed (the
   known commit-index cache size jitter excepted, and named).
5. **Cost:**
   - `prikk verify` wall time on a 256 MiB blob container and on the release-gate repository (RFC 133), release

## Security note

Both readers decide what is damage in untrusted input: an archive from elsewhere, or a store after a crash. The
primitive must be at least as strict as each reader is today. **The differential, the matrix byte-identity and the
perturbation are the evidence.** None of them may be skipped.
