## F1: the case table and the options (90 min, stop at 180). Nothing is built

**What the architect measured** (`~/.pgtmp/s-probes/s5-tag-race.sh`, `s5-tag-race.log`, `s5-tag-race-057.log`):
- **The setup:** `tag create tags/tN --target heads/main` loops in a repository while its `sync seal` of 301 claims is re-run
  up to 400 times.
- **On both binaries, the seal never completes:**

| binary | "blocked by incomplete ref publication" refusals | lock conflicts |
|---|---:|---:|
| the S5b build | 171 | 215 |
| the published 0.57.0 | 275 | 123 |

  Two or three times it says "an interrupted publication for tags/tN … run `prikk ref complete tags/tN`", for a tag that is
  still being written.
- **`verify` stays clean,** and every tag that succeeded is listed.

**The cause:** `ensure_no_incomplete_publication` (`refs.rs:223`) reads the pointer index and the ref log with no
ref-container lock. `tag create` takes no active lock. Between its pointer write and its log append, the precondition reads a
pointer that leads the log, and refuses as if a publication had been interrupted.

**Why the precondition exists** (its doc, `refs.rs:187-191`): "a publication interrupted between its pointer write and its
ref-state write leaves a ref that reads as neither old nor new, and a second writer stepping onto it would make the damage
permanent rather than resumable". **Read that reason before proposing anything** ("a prior refusal may be right at its own
time"):
- What exactly is made permanent?
- By a writer of the **same** ref only, or of any ref?
- Why is the check repository-wide today? RFC 165 (`rfcs/done/165-…md`, R2/M8), and the tests that tie it to `append_patch`,
  `add_trusted_maintainer` and `repair_repository`.

**F1 delivers:**
1. **The case table, measured on `99d05f86`.** The other process's publication is stopped at each of its points: before the
   pointer write; pointer written, log not; both written. It is stopped by a failpoint or the P2b pause hook, both alive
   (in flight) and killed (interrupted). Each row gives:
   - what the precondition answers today;
   - what `verify`, `doctor` and `ref complete` say;
   - what a second writer of the **same** ref and of **another** ref would do if allowed through.
2. **The options, at least these:**
   - **(a) distinguish in flight from interrupted** by the ref-container lock's holder (S5a's list says `tag create` holds
     `RefLock`, `RefPointerIndex` and `RefLog` per publication). A live holder means in flight: refuse naming the other
     command and "run it again", or wait. prikk's locks fail fast, so say which.
   - **(b) read under the locks:** take the pointer-index and log locks for the precondition's read. Say what that does to
     the lock order (the active lock, then `RefLock`, then `RefPointerIndex`, then `RefLog`, then the object store) and to
     fail-fast conflicts.
   - **(c) scope the block to the refs this command writes.** Another ref's interrupted publication would be reported by
     `verify` and `doctor` and would not block. This is only valid if F1's reading of the reason shows that a writer of
     another ref cannot make the damage permanent.

   For each: what changes for `sync seal`, `seal`, `commit`, `merge`, `branch`, `tag create`, `ref complete`, and the three
   entry points the doc names. Which existing tests change, and whether the refusal names something typeable in every row.
3. **The message.** Every row ends in a way out a user can type. "Use signer-backed seal retry" is not one. That generic
   sentence is the ROADMAP candidate already recorded; F may fix it where F's rows meet it.

**Stop at F1's report.** The architect rules, and the owner is asked if the ruling changes which writes block which.

## F2: the build (live 2026-11-16, after the F0/F1 review: `.git-exclude/reviewed/0.58.0-F-part-0-and-1-review-v1.md`) (90 min, stop at 180)

**The rule (option (a), ruled):**
1. **One function decides whether a ref's pointer lead is in flight or interrupted.** It looks at the ref's `RefLock` (and
   the pointer-index and log locks the other publication holds) with `lock_holder`'s judge.
   - **In flight only when the holder *appears running*.** *Unknown* or *does not appear to be running* is today's answer,
     and its message adds what the judge said about the holder (step 0a's rule).
   - **The read order:** the lead, then the holder, then the lead again. A lead that is gone on the re-read is no lead, and
     the writer proceeds.
2. **The precondition** (`ensure_no_incomplete_publication`), for a lead that is in flight:
   - **refuses before any write,** exit 1;
   - **says what is happening:** "another command is publishing tags/t1 (pid N, from its lock); run this command again
     when it finishes";
   - never "interrupted", and never names `ref complete`.

   Every writer the precondition gates gets it: `commit`, `seal`, `sync seal`, `merge`, `branch`, `tag create`, and the R2
   entry points.
3. **`ref complete <ref>`,** with a live holder on its ref, says the same thing instead of the lock-conflict text with the
   `unlock` advice.
4. **`verify` and `doctor` use the same function.**
   - A live holder makes the lead `info`: "a publication of tags/t1 is in flight (pid N)". `verify` exits 0 on it.
   - A dead or unknown holder keeps `PRIKK-VERIFY-REF-POINTER-LEADS-LOG`, exit 1. **Keep the id.**
   - **Check consumers' fixed strings first:** planeter keys on `PRIKK-*` ids. Is the in-flight `info` a new id, or the old
     id at a new severity? Propose, and do not choose silently.
5. **No wait.** Refuse, as every prikk lock does. A wait is the owner's question (`.git-exclude/scratch/0.58.0-F-what-a-is-and-is-not-v1.md`).

**First, the case table:** F1's seven rows, re-measured on the build for every writer column, with the in-flight row's new
answer. **Add two rows:**
- the holder's liveness *unknown*: a body from another PID namespace, as 0a's test writes it;
- the race: the publication finishes between the precondition's two reads.

**Tests,** through the binary:
- one per writer kind in the in-flight row (the P2b pause hook, or F1's probe hook made `test-support`);
- the unknown-holder row;
- the finished-between-reads row;
- `verify` and `doctor` in flight and after the holder dies;
- **the red control:** treat *unknown* as in flight, and the unknown-holder test goes red;
- **the red control:** drop the re-read, and the race test goes red.

**Docs:** `concurrency-locking.md` (the in-flight window and what each command says in it), `troubleshooting.md` (the
message), `integrity-recovery.md` (the finding's severity). **CHANGELOG:** `### Fixed` (a released defect: measured on the
published 0.57.0 by the architect's race, 275 false "interrupted" refusals in 400 rounds) and `### Output changes`.

**Close with the race re-run:** `~/.pgtmp/s-probes/s5-tag-race.sh` on the build. **Expect it still not to converge** while the
