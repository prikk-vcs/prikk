# 0.55.0 step 0 — round G2: the CLI's tests, in three one-sitting parts

**Live 2026-10-24, and it is next.** The owner authorized G2 on 2026-10-21 (*"Yes. Authorized."*). The owner approved
0.55.0's scope on 2026-10-24, G2 first: `.git-exclude/scratch/0.55.0-plan-proposal-v1.md`. The plan is in

**Why now, and why the care:** G2 touches about 140 test files. That is why it waited for a release's start, not its
end. **The tests must mean exactly what they meant before.** Every part proves it.

## The working rule

- **One part per sitting,** in order, with one final report each.
- **`date` at each part's start and end.** R1 scopes for every run, all 14 gates at the end of each part, and controls
  shown red.
- **Measure before you change anything** (Part A), on an idle machine, three samples each. Keep every log the report
  cites (`| tee`).
- **No test is deleted, skipped, `#[ignore]`d or weakened.**
- **The test list must be identical before and after:** `cargo test -p prikk -- --list`, in both `--release` and debug,
  sorted. Names may gain a module prefix from grouping; nothing else may differ.

## What "done" means

- the CLI's test list is unchanged, and every test passes in all 14 gates;
- the cold build and wall time are measured before and after, with the difference reported as it is. If it is not
  faster, say so: that is a result, not a failure;
- publishing `prikk` still works;
- nothing a procedure runs by name has broken.
